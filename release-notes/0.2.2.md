## Headhunter Informants

- Administrators can place persistent male or female Headhunter Informants from the Bosses radial menu.
- Players can buy a localized intelligence mail for a random active boss group in their current sector. It includes the group level, current member names, and coordinate snapshots.
- The price is configurable per live group member. Wallet payment and Mail delivery are checked safely, including an automatic refund attempt if delivery fails.

## Improvements

- Informant offer buttons correctly close the modal UI and release the mouse cursor.
- Dummy boss encounters have a broader clothing selection, with a chance for a compatible hat.
