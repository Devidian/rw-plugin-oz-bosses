## Configurable spawn boundaries

- Administrators can prevent automatic boss spawns inside Rising World Areas.
- Administrators can limit active boss groups per sector; `-1` keeps the
  previous unlimited behavior and `0` prevents new automatic groups.
- The new settings are available in PluginSettings in German and English.

Both settings are additive and preserve existing boss persistence and editable
runtime catalogs. OZ Tools 0.23.8 or newer is required.
